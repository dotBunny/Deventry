# Away from your desk

Claude can work for a long time, and you don't have to sit and watch. This chapter covers how Deventry tells you when something needs you, how the Dock or taskbar icon shows what your tabs are doing, how to reach a tab from your phone, through the Claude app or a Telegram or Discord bot of your own, how to stop the computer sleeping in the middle of a task, and how to have Deventry start when you log in.
