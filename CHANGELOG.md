# Changelog

What's changed in each version of Deventry, formerly Claudette, for the people using it.

The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and the versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html). Until 1.0.0, a new feature or a change you'd notice raises the second number (0.2.0 to 0.3.0), and fixes alone the third (0.3.0 to 0.3.1).

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- **Unload idle tabs to save memory.** Settings → General → **Unload idle tabs to save memory** stops Claude Code in a tab left idle for an hour, or as long as you choose. The tab and its conversation stay as they are, and your next message picks the session up where it was. Off by default.
- **Start a new project from the new tab picker.** **New project…** makes an empty folder where you choose, a git repository from the start unless you turn that off, and opens it as a new tab; the same window clones a repository instead with **Clone from git**. It's in the command palette too.
- **Clone a repository…**, from **New project…** in the new tab picker or from the command palette. Paste a git address, choose where to put the clone and what to call its folder (filled in from the address), and the clone opens as a new tab. It runs the git from Settings → Source control → Git, and tells you what git said if it can't. See [Cloning a repository](https://deventry.app/tabs/opening-tabs/#cloning-a-repository).
- **Show or hide ignored and hidden files in the Files tab.** The explorer's **⋯** menu has **Show ignored files** and **Show hidden files**: ticked, they're in the tree, dimmed, and unticked they're left out, in every Files tab. Hidden files are dotfiles, such as `.gitignore` and `.github`, and what Windows or macOS hides, and are now dimmed too. In a Perforce workspace, what your P4IGNORE rules ignore counts as ignored, as what git ignores does; Perforce is asked in the background, so the tree never waits for it. Both are in Settings → **Files and diffs**, where **Files git ignores** moved from Settings → Git as **Ignored files**. See [The explorer](https://deventry.app/project-files/the-explorer/#the-tree).
- **See at a glance which tabs are working.** A tab's row in the sidebar is colored by what Claude is doing in it: a soft highlight runs across it while Claude works, or its subagents still do in the background, a tab waiting for you flashes orange, a tab Claude finished in while you were elsewhere keeps a pale green wash until you open it, and one that stopped with an error a red one. A starting tab's name shimmers until it's ready. Each subagent's row under its tab is colored the same way by what the subagent is doing. The status icons go, and a tab's mark moves to the start of its second line; Settings → Appearance → **Tab activity** → **Status dots** brings them back. See [What the tab is doing](https://deventry.app/tabs/the-sidebar/#what-the-tab-is-doing).
- **See which subagents are still running.** Each subagent's row says what it's doing in the accent color while it runs, and how it ended in green once it's done. Turn on Settings → Conversation → Tab rows → **Show running subagents**, and while any of a tab's subagents works, even after Claude's turn has ended, its row in the sidebar says how many with a **running** chip; click it to see them. While the chip shows, the count of tasks still running after the turn leaves them out.
- **Find a tab by typing part of its name.** `Ctrl/Cmd+K`, the magnifier at the top of the sidebar or **Find a tab…** in the command palette opens a box that finds your open tabs by pieces of their names, their project's name, their folder or their git branch: type `login` or `orbit auth`, and tabs whose name has the words come first. Pick one with `Enter` or a click, and Deventry goes to it, opening its group if it was collapsed, scrolling the sidebar to its row and pulsing the row for a moment so you see where it is (a still highlight that fades, with motion reduced). The shortcut can be changed in Settings → Keyboard. See [Finding a tab](https://deventry.app/tabs/the-command-palette/#finding-a-tab).

- **Pipeline with Joulene.** A fifth game against her: lay pipe from her tap to her cup before her flow catches up. She hands out the pieces, and the flow has to reach her cup, in by its open side, before it spills at an open end, the edge, a rock or a pipe that doesn't take it. Her tap and her cup stand out on the board, each tinted, with an arrow out of the tap and into the cup, and the cup is further off on harder levels. A crossing takes the flow twice, once each way. Click a square, or press `Enter` on the one with the focus, to lay the next piece there, and the square shows it before you do. **Open now** opens her tap early, and **Faster** hurries the flow once it's on its way. The flow waits while the Games window is hidden or Deventry is in the background, so going off to answer Claude doesn't lose you the round. Start it from her right-click menu, the command palette, the usage limit bar or the Games window. See [Pipeline](https://deventry.app/joulene/games-with-joulene/#pipeline).

- **Group your project's actions and links, and give them icons.** In `deventry.json`, `"group": "Deploy"` puts an action under a section of that name in the project menu's **Actions**, with the others in that group, and a link likewise in its **Links**; `"icon": "upload"` gives either one of Deventry's icons. The action and link dialogs in the project's settings have **Group** and **Icon** too. See [deventry.json](https://deventry.app/reference/deventry-json/).

- **Wrap lines in the Files tab's editor.** A text file in the Files tab has **Wrap lines** in the bar under the file tabs, and so does a Markdown file in **Edit** or **Edit and preview**: on, long lines wrap at the editor's width instead of running off to the side. Each file keeps its own, and it comes back with the file's tab when Deventry starts again. `Alt+Z` (`Option+Z`) and the command palette's **Wrap the file's lines** turn the same on and off, and Settings → Files and diffs → **Wrap long lines** is where a file starts until you do. See [Wrapping long lines](https://deventry.app/project-files/editing/#wrapping-long-lines).

- **Every image, video and sound in a tab, in one place.** The **Media** window, on the strip of icons at the right once a conversation has any, gathers them into two sections you can fold: **User**, the images you attach and the files your messages name, and **Agent**, the images Claude's tools hand it (an image it read, a screenshot from a browser tool) and the image, video and sound files Claude and its subagents write, read, make with a command or name in a reply. Newest first, each once. Click one to open an image in Deventry's image window, or a video or a sound in the app your OS uses for it; right-click for **Show in conversation**, **Reveal in file manager** and **Copy path**. See [Media](https://deventry.app/conversation/media/).

- **Your tabs from your phone, through a Telegram bot.** Make a bot with @BotFather, give Deventry its token in Settings → Remote clients → **Telegram**, and pair your phone with **Pair a phone…** by scanning a QR code. The bot tells you when a tab needs you or finishes, with the start of the reply and the files it changed; answers its prompts with a tap (**Allow**, **Deny**, a question's options, a plan's approvals); sends a message to a tab when you reply to one of its messages, or to the tab `/to` picks; stops Claude; and shows your tabs (`/tabs`) and usage (`/usage`). Deventry talks to the bot itself, so none of that costs tokens but what you send a tab. By default it sends messages only while you're away: Deventry not in front, the computer locked or asleep, or nobody at it for five minutes. Only the accounts you pair are answered, the bot's token stays in your computer's credential store, and Remote clients → **All clients** chooses how much it can do and tell Telegram. See [Remote clients](https://deventry.app/away/remote-clients/).

### Changed

- **Changed files is a tree of their folders.** The files Claude changed are under their folders, each folder with how many files are in it and their lines added and removed, and folders that each hold only the next share a row, such as *Source/Starfall*. Click a folder to fold it, Alt+click to fold every folder under it too, or use **Expand all** and **Collapse all** at the top; each tab remembers the folders you collapsed. A folder's box ticks every file in it as reviewed, and is partly ticked while only some are. When Claude changed files in more than one worktree, each one heads a tree of its own. The button beside **Refresh** switches back to the list, in every tab. See [The tree](https://deventry.app/changes/changed-files/#the-tree).
- **The project's menu is in sections, with icons**, as a tab's and a group's menus are: the project's own actions under **Editor**, **Build** and **Clean up**, its configuration, and **Folder**, where your own actions and links are in submenus, **Actions** and **Links**. The project's solution from `deventry.json` is under **Editor**. **Add an action…** and **Add a link…** left it: add actions and links in **Project settings…**, from a group's or a tab's menu.
- **Selecting a tab no longer starts Claude Code.** A tab brought back when Deventry starts shows its conversation as soon as you select it, ready to read and scroll, and starts Claude Code only when you send it a message.
- **Connect Four is now Four in a Row.** The same game against Joulene, under the name it's known by everywhere else; your score and level come with it.
- **The games look like games.** Each board is one surface, with slots to play in and keys that sink when you press them, in place of rows of buttons. An empty square shows your ✕ before you play it, a column shows where your piece would land, the three in a row that win tic-tac-toe are struck through, the last piece dropped in Four in a Row has a dot, hangman's word is spelled out on lines, and Minesweeper's squares sit raised until you open them.
- **An editor log closes with its tab.** Closing a tab closes the editor logs under it, instead of moving them to another of the project's tabs. While the editor keeps running, its log stays closed, as it does when you close it with its **×**, and comes back when the editor next starts. See [Editor logs](https://deventry.app/project-tools/editor-logs/).
- **Unity's log with `-timestamps` reads in columns.** The time and thread Unity puts before each line (`2026-11-06T12:45:48.327Z|0x9484|`) are no longer part of the message: the time is in the time column, in your time zone, and the thread has a column of its own before the message, in `Editor.log` and in Unity's JSON log, where the time no longer shows twice. Copying a line still gives it as the file has it. See [Editor logs](https://deventry.app/project-tools/editor-logs/).
- **Every banner under the header can be dismissed.** The usage credits notice has **Dismiss**, which hides it until credits start paying again; so do the sign-in banner, until Claude Code needs you to sign in again (the account menu still has **Sign in**), and the demo's, for as long as the demo runs. See [When usage credits pay](https://deventry.app/usage/the-usage-header/#when-usage-credits-pay).
- **Usage alerts keep themselves up to date.** Once usage credits start paying, the session's warning and *On pace to hit the session limit* lines under the header give way to their notice, which says the limit is used up. A warning's line goes once the session resets, and a limit's reset line keeps up with the limit as it fills again. See [Alerts](https://deventry.app/usage/alerts/#where-they-show).

### Fixed

- **A tab's mark sits level with its second line, and is smaller.** In the sidebar, a tab's mark is centered on its row's second line, as the context ring at its end is, instead of hanging below the line. Every mark is drawn at the same scale, smaller than before, so the question mark no longer stands taller than the others, in the sidebar, the rail, the tab's menu and History.
- **The bar under the message box is level.** The model, effort and mode at its left sit level with **Files**, the token count and the send key at its right, in the middle of the send button, rather than a little higher.
- **A tab that hasn't started shows the mode it will start in.** A restored tab, or one unloaded while idle, said **Manual** under the message box until its first message started it in Auto, or in the mode Tab settings chose. It now shows that mode from the start, with Bypass permissions' red warning when it will start in Bypass, and Tab settings names Claude Code's default, **Default (Auto)**, before the tab starts. See [Permission modes](https://deventry.app/permissions/permission-modes/).
- **An image's outline is whole at its corners.** The rounded outline around an image you attach, an image in your message and one under a tool's card no longer has its corners covered by the image, and neither does the outline that lights up when you point at one.
- **Italics keep their last letter whole.** A preview tab's name in the Files tab, such as *the-crawl.md*, no longer loses the top of its last letter, and neither do Claude's thinking or italics in replies and in a Markdown file's preview, as in a table's cell.
- **A subagent Claude stops is shown as stopped.** One Claude stopped in the background kept running in its tab, with the tab's running tasks counting it, until the tab's session restarted.
- **After Deventry ends unexpectedly, its sessions aren't "open" on your own computer.** A session that syncs, open when Deventry crashed or was ended, no longer asks whether to take it over from this same computer when you open it from History, and its tab brought back at launch starts instead of becoming read-only. Deventry knows the claim it left on the session for its own. See [One machine at a time](https://deventry.app/history/picking-up-on-another-machine/#one-machine-at-a-time).
- **The usage credits notice no longer comes and goes.** With a limit used up and usage credits paying, the notice under the header could disappear and come back minutes later, with another *Using usage credits* notification. A request Claude Code let through past the limit some other way, from any tab, was taken to mean credits had stopped. Now only a request they were refused for, or one with no limit used up, ends the notice.
- **A read-only tab stays read-only.** A tab that became read-only because its session carried on on another machine started Claude Code again when you went back to it, or from its **Restart**, and held the session, so the other machine was told it was open here. It now stays read-only, with no **Restart**, and says to close it and open the session again from History to carry on here, since opening it from History while the tab is open only goes to the tab.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-04

**Claudette is now Deventry**, an agentic development environment (ADE) for your desktop: made for Claude Code first, with more agents to come, and light on tokens by default. The same app, with a new name, a new icon and a new mascot on the composer, Joulene. Everything comes with you: the first time Deventry starts, it takes over Claudette's tabs, settings, History, session library and saved passwords and API keys, and if Claudette started when you logged in, Deventry does instead.

### Added

- **A project's own icon for its group.** Choose a picture (a PNG, ICO, JPEG, BMP, GIF or WebP) on the **Group** page of the project's settings, and its group shows it in the sidebar in place of the colored dot. It's saved in the project's `deventry.json`, for everyone who opens it, or in `deventry.local.json`, just for you.
- **A group's color is saved with its project.** Pick it with **Choose…** on the **Group** page of the project's settings: when you close the picker, it's saved in the project's `deventry.json`, so everyone who opens the project sees its group in that color, on every machine, or in `deventry.local.json`, yours alone, which wins. **Remove** takes it out.
- **Remove past sessions from the sidebar.** With Claude Code's projects in the sidebar, point at a past session and click its **×** to take it off. To take them all at once, right-click a group showing only past sessions and choose **Remove from the sidebar**, or **Remove past sessions** for a group with tabs open, which stay. History still lists them, and one you use again comes back; Settings → Tabs → **Claude Code's projects** shows them all again. See [Removing past sessions](https://deventry.app/tabs/folder-groups/#removing-past-sessions).

- **Open a project's solution from its menu.** Name the file that defines a project, usually its solution, on the new **Solution** page of the project's settings, and the project's menu offers **Open NightOwl.sln**: a solution opens with the app Settings → Project tools → All tools says, your system's own by default, and any other file with the app your system has for it. It sits beside an Unreal, Unity or Godot project's own **Open solution**, and in any other folder it's what `Ctrl+Shift+E` (`⇧⌘E`) does. It's saved as `solution` in `deventry.json` or `deventry.local.json`. See [Project settings](https://deventry.app/settings/project-settings/).

- **Claude's answers in the scroll bar.** Settings → Conversation → **Marks in the scroll bar** → **Mark Claude's answers** marks each of Claude's answers in the conversation's scroll bar, in its card's color, to click to go back to it: its replies to you, not the ones it carried on after with a tool call. **Mark your messages** beside it turns your messages' marks off. See [Marks in the scroll bar](https://deventry.app/conversation/finding-your-way/#marks-in-the-scroll-bar).

- **Fold the Modified files card.** Click its header line, or the chevron after its count, and a turn's Modified files card folds to that line, as a Tool calls card does. Settings → Conversation → **Show modified files collapsed** starts them folded, and right-clicking one gives **Collapse all modified files** and **Expand all modified files** for the tab. See [Folding the files](https://deventry.app/conversation/the-end-of-a-turn/#folding-the-files).

- **Unity's JSON log.** When Unity 6 writes its JSON log, `Editor.jsonl`, with its logging framework's JSON logging on, the tab shows that one in place of `Editor.log`: each entry with its time, category and level in columns, its stack trace under it, and the log's **Categories** filter for the categories it names. Settings → Project tools → Unity → **Prefer JSON logging**, on to begin with, turns it off. See [Unity's JSON log](https://deventry.app/project-tools/editor-logs/#unitys-json-log).

- **Find in a Markdown file's preview.** `Ctrl+F` (`⌘F`) in the preview finds text in it, as in the editor: every match marked, the one you're on outlined, *3 of 12*, `Enter` and `Shift+Enter` to go from one to the next, and **Aa**, **W** and **.\*** for match case, whole words and regular expressions. In **Edit and preview** it finds on whichever side has the keyboard. See [Finding in the preview](https://deventry.app/project-files/markdown-files/#finding-in-the-preview).

### Changed

- **Restart into new builds by itself is in Settings too.** A source build's updates in Settings → General have the switch that **New build ready**'s dialog has, so it can be turned off without catching the badge before the next restart.
- **Tabs don't spend usage credits unless you say so.** Once your session or weekly limit is used up, a tab now stops and waits for the reset rather than let your plan's usage credits pay for it. Turn on Settings → Tabs → **Use usage credits once a limit is used up** for every tab, or tick **Use usage credits** in a tab's menu for the ones that can't wait. Settings carried over from Claudette keep it on, as it was. See [Tabs that don't use usage credits](https://deventry.app/usage/tabs-that-dont-use-usage-credits/).
- **The Games icon is always on the strip.** While games are on, its icon stays at the window's right, game or no game, so you can start one at any time: with none open, its window lists the four, each with your score so far. **Done playing** goes back to that list. See [Games with Joulene](https://deventry.app/joulene/games-with-joulene/).
- **Project settings have a window of their own.** **Project settings…**, in a group's menu and now in each tab's, opens the project's links, actions, tools, and its group's color and icon in a window beside the main one, one for each project, even for a group with nothing open in it. **Add a link…** and **Add an action…** open it too. They're no longer at the foot of Settings' sidebar, and a group's color is picked there now, in place of **Change color…** in the group's menu. See [Project settings](https://deventry.app/settings/project-settings/).
- **The project's menu for a folder with nothing open.** A group in the sidebar with no tab open, such as one of Claude Code's projects showing only its past sessions, now has its project's icon and menu, in place of a button that opened the folder: launch the editor, open the solution or a link from there. Builds and other actions with output need a tab open to run in. See [A folder with no tab open](https://deventry.app/project-tools/using-project-tools/#a-folder-with-no-tab-open).
- **A new name: Deventry**, in its windows, menus, notifications and installers. On Windows it updates the Claudette you have. On a Mac, Claudette can't update itself to Deventry, so install it by hand this once: download it, drag `Deventry.app` to Applications and `Claudette.app` to the Trash. Everything still comes with you, though macOS asks again whether Deventry may send notifications and use your folders, and, for each password and API key Claudette saved, whether Deventry may use it: choose **Always Allow**. Its website is [deventry.app](https://deventry.app), and the old address takes you there.
- **A new icon**: a road-work sign in Joulene's blue, with a prompt on it. On a Mac, while Claude works, the prompt becomes an arrow board's chevrons, lighting in turn; while a usage limit holds a task, the sign shows an hourglass; and while a tab needs you, it shows a `?` and a `!` by turns. On the Windows taskbar, the chevrons lighting take the place of Claude's spark, and with the hourglass they're in Joulene's blue on a light taskbar and white on a dark one.
- **Joulene on the composer**, in place of Claudette: a medieval lady with platinum hair, a gold circlet and a blue gown. She does everything Claudette did, and now and then visits her cave: it appears beside her, she walks in, peeks out and comes back to wave. Her settings are in Settings → Appearance → **Joulene**, as you had them, and the gem in her circlet takes the colour of the tab's group, as Claudette's hair tie did.
- **`deventry.json`** holds a project's actions and links. A project's `claudette.json` and `claudette.local.json` still work, and Deventry saves to them; a project with neither gets `deventry.json`.
- **`DEVENTRY_` environment variables.** Project actions get `DEVENTRY_WORKTREE` and `DEVENTRY_MAIN_CHECKOUT`, and your login shell `DEVENTRY_RESOLVING_ENVIRONMENT`, beside their `CLAUDETTE_` names for now. `CLAUDETTE_HOME` still works too.
- **A web page Claude names opens in your browser.** A link in a reply, or a file's name in its code, that's an `.html` page opens in your default browser rather than in Deventry's editor, and so does one linked from a Markdown file's preview. Changed files' and a tool card's **Open** still open it in the editor, to work on it.
- **In the Microsoft Store**, Deventry has a listing of its own. Install it from there, and everything carries over as it does from a download.
- **A tab says why it won't start** when one of Claude Code's configuration files isn't valid JSON, such as `~/.claude.json` after a full disk, or when a sandbox your settings require can't run, with what to do.
- Tested with Claude Code 2.1.318.

### Fixed

- **Closing a tab no longer asks about its MCP servers.** A server Claude Code runs as a program, such as Unity's `relay_win`, was listed as a process the tab started, so a tab with one in your settings asked *Close and stop it* every time it closed. MCP servers are Claude Code's own and stop with it; the question now lists only what Claude started, such as a dev server.

## [0.7.0] - 2026-10-31

**This is the last version called Claudette.** The next one, 1.0.0, is **Deventry**: the same app with a new name, and a new mascot on the composer, Joulene. When Deventry is released, this version offers it as an update like any other, and your tabs, settings, History and saved passwords come with you; on a Mac, it arrives in Applications as `Deventry.app`, in place of `Claudette.app`. If you installed Claudette from the Microsoft Store, Deventry has a listing of its own there to install it from.

### Added

- **A warning before a session moves to another account.** Resuming a conversation under a different account than the one it started under now asks first, since the whole earlier conversation would go to that account, whose organization may be able to see it: continue, go back to the original account, start a new chat, or start a new chat with a short digest you can edit before sending. Settings → Accounts can turn it off. See [Moving a tab to another account](https://claudette.dotbunny.com/accounts/moving-a-tab-to-another-account/#a-conversation-started-under-another-account).
- **Deventry remembers what your organization turned off.** When your organization has turned Remote Control off for your account, Deventry now accepts it: no tab tries to connect on later launches, so the same yellow warning doesn't come back, and the tab's switch says it's your organization's rule for that account, since when. Settings → Accounts → **What an organization has turned off** lists these rules, each with **Remove** for when something changes. Another account is not affected. See [The Claude app](https://claudette.dotbunny.com/away/the-claude-app/#who-cant-use-it).
- **Try Claudette without an account.** **Try the demo**, on the sign-in screen and on the screen that offers to install Claude Code, opens Claudette in a window of its own on made-up game projects, with a stand-in for Claude that answers offline. Its tabs, History, the usage header and an API-key account are there to look around, and a message gets an example turn: thinking, a file it asks your permission to write, or a question. Nothing in it reaches Claude or your account, and **Leave demo** closes it, taking what it made with it. See [Trying the demo](https://claudette.dotbunny.com/getting-started/trying-the-demo/).
- **Open a file in a window of its own.** In a Files tab, right-click a file's tab and choose **Open in a window**, click the window button at the right of the tabs' row, or drag the tab out of Claudette's window, and the file opens in a window you can put on another screen or beside Claudette, with its unsaved edits, Claude's changes and the Files tab's keys. Its tab stays in the row, marked, and clicking it brings the window forward, as does opening the file from the conversation. **Put back**, in the window's toolbar or on the tab's menu, or closing the window, returns the file to its tab. The next time Claudette starts, the file is back in its tab. See [File tabs](https://claudette.dotbunny.com/project-files/file-tabs/#in-a-window-of-its-own).
- **Pin a project to the top of the sidebar.** **Pin to the top** in a group's menu puts the group first, so the projects you're always in are never to be found by scrolling. Pinned groups sit in a shaded band of their own at the top, with a line between them and the rest. It works on Claude Code's projects with nothing open too. See [Pinning a group](https://claudette.dotbunny.com/tabs/folder-groups/#pinning-a-group).
- **Claude Code's projects in the sidebar.** Settings → Tabs → **Show Claude Code's projects in the sidebar** gives every folder Claude Code has sessions in on this machine a group in the sidebar, tab or no tab, so you work in your projects straight from the sidebar, as you would from their folders in a terminal. A group with nothing open lists the folder's latest sessions, with when each was last used and how many messages; click one to pick it up in a new tab, or **Show all in History…**. Once a tab is open in the group, the rest fold away under it. The sessions are the ones History lists, Claude Code's own on this machine and the session library's. The first time, Claudette asks whether to show them; **Not now** leaves the switch in Settings. See [Claude Code's projects](https://claudette.dotbunny.com/tabs/folder-groups/#claude-codes-projects).
- **Fold each chain of steps.** Settings → Conversation → **Fold each chain of steps** folds a whole run of Claude's thinking and tool calls between two messages into one, such as *7 steps · 18m 43s*: on the row of the task it was for, in the header of Claude's reply after it, or a line of its own. Each tab can fold its steps or show them all for itself, from **Steps** in its menu or its **Tab settings…**, and while the setting is on, from **Steps** in the message box's bar. A click opens the steps inside the reply's card, under its text, without moving it on screen: a list, a line each (*Thought for 7s*, *Ran check for UE4SS.pdb*, *Edited disasm.py*), which scrolls once it's long, and each line opens further, to what Claude thought or what the call did. While Claude is still on the run, its last step stays under the line. See [Tool calls](https://claudette.dotbunny.com/conversation/tool-calls/#folding-a-chain-of-steps).
- **Collapse the tool calls.** A click anywhere on a Tool calls card's header line, which lights from end to end under the pointer as a thinking row's does, collapses the card to that line, and another brings its tiles back, so a long run of calls takes one line. As with thinking, Settings → Conversation → **Show tool calls collapsed** starts new cards collapsed, and right-clicking a card gives **Collapse all tool calls** and **Expand all tool calls** for the tab. See [Tool calls](https://claudette.dotbunny.com/conversation/tool-calls/#folding-the-calls).
- **Set your messages apart from Claude's.** Settings → Conversation → **Indent your messages** starts your messages' cards far in from the left, with Claude's replies at the left edge, so you can tell whose turn a card is from its place alone, as you can in a chat. **Indent Claude's steps** starts its thinking, tool calls and subagents a little in from its replies. Both are off until you turn them on. See [Reading the conversation](https://claudette.dotbunny.com/conversation/reading-the-conversation/#indent-your-messages).

- A screen reader read the rows of Changed files, the command palette, the new tab picker, a Files tab's explorer and file finder, the diff view and a project's links and actions by Claudette's internal names for them. It now reads each by what it shows, such as a file's name and how it changed.
- The group menu in the collapsed sidebar didn't have **Open project files**.
- Messages that only tell you something, such as a worktree that couldn't be removed, offered **Cancel** beside **OK**.
- Many smaller slips in Claudette's wording: plurals such as *1 turns*, *From the session another session*, a turn that took *1m 60s*, the header's cup saying twice that it can't keep the computer awake, exit codes written three ways, and settings named by labels they no longer have.

## [0.5.1] - 2026-10-23

A badge at the foot of the sidebar says *3 new commits on origin/main* and lists them; **Pull, build and restart** pulls them, builds Claudette and restarts into it with every tab as it was, and **Update when idle** waits for your tabs to finish first. It only ever fast-forwards: with changes or commits of your own in the checkout, it tells you why it won't pull and leaves the checkout alone.

### Changed

- Tested with Claude Code 2.1.308.

### Fixed

- **Find in the conversation** brought the message into view but didn't show where in it the text was. The text you searched for is now highlighted in the message, whether it's in a reply, a code block, your prompt or a tool's output.
- **Files** under the composer opened the side panel on whichever page it last showed, and closed it when it showed another. It now always opens it on Changed files, and closes it only from there.
- The Windows package wasn't signed, so Windows wouldn't install it. It's now signed by dotBunny.
- A long to-do list at the top of the conversation could fill a short window and push the conversation out of sight. Now it takes at most two fifths of the conversation's height and scrolls past that.

## [0.5.0] - 2026-10-22

### Added

- **Editor logs under the tab.** While the Unreal editor, a commandlet or a game run has the tab's project open, its log is a row under the tab, and a click shows it in the main area as a project run's log is, followed as it's written, with its errors and warnings colored and counted on the row. Unreal's lines show in columns: the time in your computer's clock, the log category, the verbosity and the message. A filter over the log hides levels with a click on their chips, each with its count, Unreal's categories from a menu, or every line without some text; the levels and categories you hide are remembered for the project. Unity's editor and batch runs, and Godot's editor and game, get theirs too. The row stays after the editor closes, says *Crashed* when its log ends in a crash, and comes back with the new log when the editor starts again. Claudette reads the file without keeping it open, so the editor can rename or delete it as it always does. **Show editor logs under the tab** in Settings → Project tools turns it off.
- **Collapse a tab in the sidebar.** A tab with rows under it, a thread's sub-threads, subagents or a project action's runs, has a chevron beside its name that folds them away, as a project group's does; **Collapse tab** in its menu or the command palette does the same. The collapsed row says how many it hides and shows the most urgent of them, such as a sub-thread waiting on you or a failed build. It opens again when you go to one of them, when a sub-thread joins, or when one starts waiting on you, and it stays collapsed when Claudette restarts.
- **Zoom in the image window.** Turn the mouse wheel over an image to zoom in and out about the pointer, or use **+** and **−** in the window's header or the `+`, `-` and `0` keys; **Fit** shows the whole image again. It goes from 10% to 1600%, and once it's larger than the window you can drag it around with the left mouse button, holding `Space` or not, or the middle one.

### Changed

- **Close the to-do list at the top of the conversation.** Its line has a chevron, and once you close it the list stays closed while Claude works through it, and when Claudette restarts, rather than opening again each time Claude changes it. Closed, the line names the task in progress, so a long list takes one line. It opens by itself when Claude starts a new list.
- **Stay current and Wrap lines over a project action's log.** **Stay current** shows whether the log is following its newest line: scroll up and it turns off, so the log holds still while you read; scroll back to the end, or click it, and it follows again. Turn **Wrap lines** off and long lines stay on one line, scrolling sideways. Each new log starts as you left the last one.

### Fixed

- When a long conversation opened, such as a resumed session's, its newest message shook up and down by a pixel for a few seconds while Claudette laid out the messages above it, and could drop down for a moment as it started.

## [0.4.0] - 2026-10-18

### Added

- **`@` completes any path.** Type a folder after `@` and the list shows what's in it, from `C:\`, `/`, `~/` or `../` too, folder by folder, and picks a file outside the project in full. A folder in the project shows the files git ignores as well, dimmed, and the tab's extra folders' files are in the list with the project's.
- **Subagents under their tab.** Each subagent Claude starts has a row of its own under its tab, nested under the one that started it and dimmed once it's finished and from an earlier turn. Select one to see its work in the tab, with the prompt it was given and what it handed back; the conversation keeps a one-line card for it. You can't write to a subagent, so **Message it through Claude** names it in your next message instead, and a permission prompt it waits on is answered right there. The Agents page opens a subagent's work the same way. To open subagents' cards in the conversation as before, set Settings → Appearance → **Show subagents' work** to **In the conversation**.
- **Images open in Claudette.** Click an image file Claude names (a PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP or BMP), its **Open** on a tool card or in Changed files, or a link to an image on the web, and it opens in Claudette's image window rather than another app. In Project Files, double-click an image, or use **Open in Claudette's image viewer** where a file says it isn't text. To open them in your system's app as before, set Settings → Editor → **Open images in** to **The app the OS uses for the file**.
- **Animated GIFs play.** An animated GIF or WebP plays in the image window, with **Pause** and **Play**. With motion reduced, it opens still until you press **Play**.
- **Discourage subagents, or turn them off.** Settings → Claude Code → **Subagents**, or one tab's **Tab settings…**: **Discourage them** asks Claude to do the work itself unless you ask for a subagent, and **Don't allow them** turns down any it starts, in tabs already open too. Claude hears about a change with your next message.

### Changed

- **A project action's log shows where the conversation is.** Clicking an action's entry under its tab shows its log in the main area, with room to read a build's errors, and the tab's own row takes you back to the conversation. **Open beside** in the entry's menu, or dragging it onto the conversation, puts the log beside it. Web addresses in the log open in your browser, and the files and folders it names open with a click, in Claudette's editor at the line an error points to. Select and copy any of it, and `Ctrl/Cmd+F` finds in it. **Show output…** in the project's menu and the *action finished* notification open it there too, and the side panel's **Project** page keeps the project's details.

### Fixed

- Just as Claude finished a turn, turning Remote Control on or off, moving the tab to another account, changing its extra folders, ticking **Sync to other machines**, or sending a message with *Queue them* chosen could wait for a turn that had already ended. A queued message, or a sub-thread's report, that went as the turn ended could leave the tab saying Claude was idle while it worked.
- When Claude Code started a turn by itself, such as for a message from the Claude app, a message sent with *Queue them* chosen, or **Auto-continue**'s message once a usage limit reset, could go in that moment as if Claude were idle.
- As the working line's glyph twinkled, the line could nudge up and down, taking the conversation above it along. On Windows, one of its stars also came out smaller and thinner than the rest.
- After Claudette restarted, such as for an update, a subagent Claude ran in the background could read *Didn't finish* when it had finished while Claude was still working.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-10-16

### Added

- **Project Files**: a tab for each project with an explorer and a code editor, opened with the file button beside a group's **+**, or **Go to file…** (`Ctrl/Cmd+P`). Find a file by name, or make a new file or folder from the explorer's menus, then edit it and save it; unsaved edits are kept when Claudette closes. Claude's changes are marked in the gutter, with a peek to see what was there and **Revert** it. A file named in the conversation opens in it at the line, beside the conversation, which gets its width back when you close the file. The diff window's **Open** brings it in front of the diff, which closes. Settings → Editor sets it up, or sends files to the OS's app instead, and **Open in the diff window** can open the file in the OS's app, or the changes in your diff tool.
- **API-key accounts.** Add a key in Settings → Accounts → **Add an API key**; it's kept only in the OS's credential store. A project group's **Account ▸** menu picks the account its new tabs use, and a tab's moves it to another, saying first what the move costs. While one of its tabs is selected, the header shows the account's estimated spend today, this week and this month, and Settings → Accounts can warn when a day or a month passes an amount.
- **Files named in Claude's replies open.** A Markdown link to a file, or a file's name in code (`Player.cpp:42`), opens the file with a click. Several files with that name are offered in a menu.
- **Tabs side by side.** **Open beside** in a tab's menu, or drag its row onto the conversation: up to three tabs at once, each with its own conversation and composer. Drag the line between two to share the width differently. A thread's **Thread ▸** → **Show with its sub-threads** shows it beside its sub-threads.
- **Conversation cards.** Each step is a card in its kind's color. A run of tool calls is one card of tiles, each opening its detail or a window of its own, and a turn ends with its **Modified files** and a **Turn** card. Marks beside the scroll bar show your messages, waiting prompts and errors; `F3` and `F4` go to the previous and next message, and `Ctrl/Cmd+F3` and `Ctrl/Cmd+F4` to the first and the latest.
- **Quieter in-between steps.** Tool calls, thinking, subagents and the other steps on the way to an answer lose their card's tint and outline, keeping the stripe, until you point at one, so your messages and Claude's answers stand out. Settings → Appearance → **Quieter in-between steps** turns it off.
- **Give Claude a task list.** Newer models keep track of their work without a written list, so the Tasks page showed only the plan. Settings → Claude Code → **Give Claude a task list**, on unless you turn it off, gives every model one, at the cost of a little room in the context. With no tasks, the status line under the composer says *Following the plan* once a plan is approved.
- **Renaming a folder group**, for folders with the same name in different places: **Rename…** in the group's menu, and **Reset name** to go back to the folder's name.
- **Using usage credits.** Once a limit is used up and your plan's usage credits pay for Claude, a notice under the header says which limit, when it resets, and what you've spent this month, with **Manage on claude.ai**. It stays until they stop paying, and an OS notification tells you when they start.
- **Tabs that don't use usage credits.** Untick **Use usage credits** in a tab's menu, or turn off Settings → Usage → **Use usage credits once a limit is used up** for every tab, and once a limit is used up the tab stops and waits for it to reset instead of spending them, so credits go only to the work that matters. A message you send meanwhile waits for the reset, with **Send now** to send it anyway.
- **The prompt cache's timer.** *Cache 52m* beside the context says how long your next message can read the conversation from Claude's cache, and turns amber in its last three minutes. Settings → Appearance → **Show when the prompt cache expires** turns it off.
- **Games with Claudette.** Tic-tac-toe, Connect Four and hangman, from her right-click menu, the command palette, or **Play a game while you wait** when a usage limit stops a task. Settings → Appearance → **Games with Claudette** turns them off.
- **Clear usage history on every machine…** in Settings → Usage, while **Share usage with my other machines** is on.
- **User guide**, under **Settings** at the foot of the sidebar and at the foot of the Settings sidebar, opens Claudette's guide in your browser.
- A turn's changes to a file open in your diff tool too, from the diff window's **Open in diff tool**, and its menu has **Open in the OS's app**.

The Usage window's **Projects** adds up what every machine spent on a project, its tabs and the busiest tabs list another machine's as *Fix the loader · Laptop*, and an API-key account's spend and its warnings count its turns on your other machines under an account of the same name.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-14

### Added

- **Threads.** Make a tab a thread from its **Thread ▸** menu, and its Claude hands work to the other tabs in its project, its sub-threads, and gets one report back when they've finished. You approve each message first, unless **Ask before sending to sub-threads** is off, and answer the sub-threads' prompts from a strip over the thread's composer.
- **Following a plan.** The Tasks page shows Claude's plan while it writes it in Plan mode, keeps every version with **Changes from** the one before, and can take a reply as the plan. Each task says who worked on it, how long it took, and the files and tokens it used. A tab's row shows how far its tasks have got (`3/7`), and a turn that ends with tasks left offers **Continue**.
- **Sessions that know each other.** Each tab's session goes by the tab's name in Claude Code (Settings → General → **Name each tab's session in Claude Code after the tab**), so other sessions can message it by name. `@` in the composer names another tab, another Claude Code session on this machine, or one of the tab's subagents. The tab's menu has **Copy session ID**, and its info card **Reached as**.
- **Claudette on the composer**, who climbs onto the message box and reacts to what the tab is doing. Settings → Appearance → **Show Claudette on the composer** turns her off.
- **Claude Docs.** A doc Claude writes on claude.ai with the Docs connector gets a link on its card, a row with **Open** at the end of the turn, and a place on the Tasks page.
- **Images from Claude's tools** show under their cards: an image Claude read, a PDF's pages, a notebook's figures, an MCP tool's screenshot. A click opens one full size, with **Copy image**, **Save as…** and **Open**.
- **Prompt suggestions.** Settings → Claude Code → **Suggest my next prompt** shows what Claude Code thinks you'll send next in the empty composer; `Tab` takes it.
- **Keep this computer awake** in Settings → General, while Claude has work to finish or whenever Claudette is running, and the cup in the header that turns it on and off.
- The button at the header's right shows and hides the side panel, and its pages can be dragged into the order you like (or **Move left**, **Move right** and **Reset order**).
- History has a chip for each recent project, to list only its sessions, and a group's menu has **History for this folder**.
- The Processes page collapses a process's children, counting them and their CPU and memory on its row, with **Expand all** and **Collapse all**.

### Changed

- Claudette moved to [dotBunny/Claudette](https://github.com/dotBunny/Claudette), and its packages are dotBunny's. 0.1.0 announces 0.2.0 but can't install it, so install 0.2.0 by hand; on Windows it installs beside 0.1.0.

### Fixed

- A link clicked in a reply opens again.
- When the last tab in a worktree reached through a symbolic link closes, Claudette offers to remove the worktree.

### Security

- A reply's images no longer load by themselves. An image from the web, or a file outside the tab's folder, waits for **Load image**, so a reply can't send anything out in an image's address, and an image on a network path never loads.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-08

The first public release.

### Added

- **Tabs**, each a Claude Code session running the real `claude` with your settings, `CLAUDE.md`, MCP servers and hooks, grouped by folder in a sidebar with each group's color. Status icons, pins, marks and the reviewed badge, tabs restored at launch, the command palette (`Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+P`) and the next tab waiting for you (`Ctrl/Cmd+J`).
- **Worktree tabs**, each in a git worktree and branch of its own, with setup actions for each new worktree and cleaning up the ones you've merged; and extra folders a tab's Claude may also use.
- **The conversation**: replies in Markdown, tool calls with their diffs and output, subagents, thinking, hook runs, find (`Ctrl/Cmd+F`), exporting to Markdown or a web page, and going back to an earlier message with **Edit and resend…**, **Branch from here** and **Restore files to before this…**. The working line says what Claude is doing, and a chip counts the tasks it keeps running.
- **The composer**: `/` commands and `@` files, attachments and large pastes, earlier prompts, drafts and the stash (`Ctrl/Cmd+S`), messages queued while Claude works, quick suffixes, the model, effort and permission mode, **Ultracode** and output styles, and check-ins on long turns.
- **Permission prompts** as cards, with **Always allow** rules you can edit, questions, plans to approve, MCP servers' forms, and a question before using a new folder's own configuration.
- **Changes to review**: the Changed files page, a diff view, ticking files reviewed, reverting a change or a whole file, and your own diff tool.
- **Usage**: the session and weekly meters with a trendline and a projection, a detailed header with charts, alerts, a Usage window with each project's tokens and cost, the context window and **Compact now**, continuing a task when a usage limit resets, and usage shared between your machines.
- **History** of every session, and a session library in a synced folder that lets another machine continue the tabs you choose, with Claudette's settings synced too.
- The **agent map** of Claude's subagents, the **Tasks** page, the **MCP** page, the **Processes** page and a **scratch pad** for each project.
- **Project tools** for Unreal Engine, Unity and Godot, your own actions and links in `claudette.json`, and Perforce logins kept fresh, with each tab's changelist.
- **The Claude app**: connect a tab to use it from your phone or claude.ai. Notifications, the Dock or taskbar badge, starting at login, and Claude's service status in the header.
- **Updates** to Claude Code, and to Claudette itself.
- The Standard and Claude styles, in light and dark, with compact density, zoom, reduced motion and screen readers' announcements.
- Packages for Windows (MSIX) and macOS (`.dmg`), for x64 and Arm.
